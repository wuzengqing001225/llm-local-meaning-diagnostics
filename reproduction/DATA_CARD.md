# Data card

Public source-derived and synthetic experiment records are grouped by corpus. CUAD has 2,174 extracted term records and 24,880 candidate occurrences, a 6,529-question scored frame, and a stratified 1,500-question validation. A separate definition-anchored CUAD task has 671 questions. Synthetic uncertainty has 763 questions and 7,630 sampled letters, with a 457-question proxy overlap. Reddit has 1,391 questions across 35 communities. DeFi has 379 questions.

The 1,500-question CUAD sample draws 300 from each of five risk strata. Its unweighted failure rate is not a population prevalence estimate. Inverse inclusion weights describe the scored frame only. RAG experiments use term-level risk aggregation and constructed questions. Definition-length counts in the historical RAG records are regex units, not provider billing tokens.

Questions and semantic categories are predominantly model-generated. Their presence is not a claim of expert verification. An author reviewed 27 deliberately selected historical questions against available sources. Subsequent source reconciliation found 10 supported stored keys, 14 invalid or source-insufficient questions, and three borderline items; because selection enriched model disagreements and stratified controls, these counts are not a population defect rate. Any blank annotation templates remain templates. Recovered response files preserve task and condition coverage. The synthetic GPT-6 usual-gloss recovery covers 441/457 archived items, while bare and local-definition responses cover all 457. Sonnet term summaries are not expanded into invented per-question labels.

CUAD source text derives from the CUAD release. Reddit glossary entries derive from Lucy and Bamman (2021), with community-context snippets used by the original experiment. DeFi examples derive from protocol documentation. Synthetic collection and structural twin are separately labelled. Third-party text retains its source terms. See `SOURCE_CATALOG.json` for per-file placement and hashes.

Private notebook text, its outputs, live credentials, and local service configuration are excluded. The 27 reviewed per-item answers are included in a separate English record; the sealed historical key ledger and per-item model-screen judgements are not. Development experiments on independent questions are included for inspection but do not establish a confirmed independent-request benefit.
