\section{Scoring diagnostic items with a small model}
\label{sec:index}
Building evaluations of this failure requires finding the questions on which models misread. We score each diagnostic question with a small open model. Let $z$ be a question with its source occurrence and keyed answer $a^*$, and let $p_S^0(a^*\mid z)$ be the probability that a proxy model $S$ assigns to the keyed option when the definition is absent. The score is
\begin{equation}
R_S(z)=1-p_S^0(a^*\mid z).
\label{eq:risk}
\end{equation}
A high $R_S$ means that the proxy gives little support to the answer the source implies. $R_S$ needs the answer key, so it applies to corpus questions whose keys come from the source, not to incoming requests. It requires no call to the model being evaluated and is computed once per question set. We evaluate $R_S$ by how well it ranks other models' errors on the same questions. The appendix also reports $\Delta p_S(z)=p_S^d(a^*\mid z)-p_S^0(a^*\mid z)$, the change in support when a definition text $d$ is prepended, which tests a given wording rather than a question (Appendix~\ref{app:statistics}). Confidence intervals resample contracts for CUAD, communities for Reddit, and term records otherwise \citep{significance}.
