\section{A small model ranks difficult diagnostic items}
\label{sec:predict}
\paragraph{Ranking other readers' errors.}
Given the answer key, $R$ from Qwen2.5-7B ranks DeepSeek V4 Flash and GPT-6-astra errors on the 456 synthetic questions with AUROC 0.848 and 0.809, and GPT-5.6-terra errors on the 1,500 CUAD validation questions with 0.815 (Table~\ref{tab:prediction}). On the exploratory Reddit and DeFi sets it reaches 0.720 to 0.809. This is comparable to using another frontier model's observed answers on the same questions, which give 0.640 to 0.892 (Appendix~\ref{app:reader-difficulty}), while term frequency and rarity reach at most 0.63. The signal grows with proxy size, from 0.699 at 0.5B parameters to 0.848 at 7B and 0.867 at 14B on synthetic questions.

\paragraph{What the score measures.}
$R$ captures general question difficulty rather than local-meaning errors specifically. On synthetic terms that keep their usual meaning its AUROC is 0.921 for DeepSeek V4 Flash and 0.846 for GPT-6-astra, based on 21 and 6 errors, and on Reddit control words it is 0.842 and 0.815 (Appendix~\ref{app:synthetic-control}). We therefore use $R$ as a low-cost screener of difficult items for building and auditing evaluation sets. It is not a detector of local-meaning misreadings, and since it needs an answer key it cannot route incoming requests.

\paragraph{Stratifying a contract library.}
We score 6,529 diagnostic questions from 303 CUAD contracts and ask GPT-5.6-terra 300 questions from each of five score bins. Observed error rises monotonically from 1.0\% in the lowest bin to 52.3\% in the highest (Figure~\ref{fig:strata}a). Reweighted to the full library, error is 13.2\% and AUROC is 0.846. The two highest bins hold 23\% of the questions and 66\% of the estimated errors, so an evaluation built from these bins concentrates the errors that a uniform sample would dilute.

\paragraph{Robustness to question quality.}
Model-drafted keys can be wrong. An author answered 27 questions from the source before seeing the stored keys, chosen as the 17 on which the two screening models disagreed and 10 stratified controls. After reconciliation with the sources, 10 keys are supported, 14 questions are invalid or not answerable from the preserved source, and 3 are borderline (Appendix~\ref{app:source-screen}). Removing the 14 invalid questions changes the AUROC of $R$ by at most 0.002 in all eight reader settings and the CUAD library AUROC from 0.846 to 0.847, and leaves the synthetic count of 93 stable errors unchanged. The released sets carry these audit labels.
