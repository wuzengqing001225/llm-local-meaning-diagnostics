\section{Limitations and conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
The diagnostic questions are drafted by models and are forced-choice. They show that errors occur and can be ranked, but they are not requests written by users. Reddit and DeFi answers more often rest on material beyond the preserved source, so those corpora are exploratory, and the main estimates come from synthetic and CUAD questions. The separation between following and recognizing a definition rests on the human-written redefinition items and on synthetic term classes fixed before generation. Within these limits, frontier models misread locally defined terms often, stably, and without signaling it, although they follow such definitions when given. Definitions written from the source repair these errors, and evaluations must include questions that reach definition boundaries.
