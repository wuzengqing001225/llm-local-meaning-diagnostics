\section{Setting}
\label{sec:meaning}
\paragraph{Local meaning and meaning-dependent questions.}
A \emph{local meaning} is the meaning or scope that a document or community assigns to a term. A \emph{reader} is a model that receives text and a question and answers it, and its \emph{default reading} is its behavior when the local definition is absent. A \emph{meaning-dependent question} asks about one occurrence of a term, and its correct answer follows from the local definition together with the occurrence. A wrong answer can arise in three ways (Table~\ref{tab:cases}). The usual meaning can conflict with the local one, the local scope can be unknown although the usual concept fits, or the needed information can lie outside the definition. A risk score that ranks all three is useful for triage, and interventions with and without the definition show which errors a definition fixes.

\paragraph{Corpora.}
We use four corpora with different sources of local meaning. A synthetic collection of invented trading-desk and restaurant vocabulary controls which terms are redefined common words, which keep their usual meaning, and which are coinages. CUAD contains 510 commercial contracts with explicit definition clauses \citep{cuad}. Reddit contains 35 communities whose members maintain glossaries of community terms \citep{lucy-bamman-glossaries}. DeFi contains developer documentation for a decentralized exchange protocol. Diagnostic questions are four-option multiple-choice items drafted by a model from the source. The synthetic drafter saw only usage sentences, while the Reddit and DeFi drafters also saw the source definition. Appendix~\ref{app:sources} gives counts and construction rules, and Table~\ref{tab:setting-design} lists the definition text used in each setting.

The synthetic and CUAD sources fully specify each answer, since the synthetic world is written from explicit specifications and contracts state their definitions. Reddit and DeFi answers often also rest on community practice or on documentation beyond the preserved excerpt. When two models of different families answered 20 hashed questions per corpus from the preserved source alone, both supported the stored key for 18 synthetic and 13 CUAD questions and for 3 Reddit and 5 DeFi questions (Appendix~\ref{app:source-screen}). The main estimates therefore come from synthetic and CUAD questions, and Reddit and DeFi results are exploratory. The central separation between following and recognizing a definition does not rest on these keys. It comes from human-written redefinition items and from synthetic term classes fixed before generation.

\paragraph{Readers.}
The readers are \texttt{gpt-5.6-terra}, \texttt{gpt-6-astra}, and DeepSeek Flash (V4 for synthetic questions and V4.1 for later runs). Jev \citep{jev} is a decision model that returns a probability for each option together with a native confidence, and we use it to test whether a confidence signal built for this purpose detects the errors. Proxies are open Qwen2.5 models from 0.5B to 14B parameters and Llama-3.1-8B \citep{qwen,llama}, run locally. Appendix~\ref{app:models} gives versions and decoding settings.
