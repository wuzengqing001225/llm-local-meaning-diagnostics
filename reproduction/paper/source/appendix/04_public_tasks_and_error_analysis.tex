The CUAD extraction comparison uses 1,661 matched questions, and adding definitions changes token F1 by at most 0.3 points. ContractNLI \citep{contractnli} uses 3,060 hypotheses with document clustering. Adding all matched notes raises accuracy by 1.1 points with an interval above zero, and gated notes by 0.6 points. Both tasks ask what a clause states rather than whether a case falls inside a defined term, so few items depend on a definition's boundary. The Reddit human-post comparison contains 178 post-term items from 176 posts. Source posts and responses are human-authored, while the evaluated multiple-choice questions are pipeline-produced. The TechQA check contains 105 drafted questions on 20 screened terms rather than the complete human-authored support task.
