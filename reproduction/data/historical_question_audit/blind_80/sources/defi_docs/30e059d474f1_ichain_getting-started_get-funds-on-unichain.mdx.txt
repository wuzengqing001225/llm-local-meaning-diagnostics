Bridge Funds with Uniswap The easiest way to get funds on Unichain is to bridge from Ethereum mainnet in the Uniswap interface. Connect your wallet, select ETH as the input token on Ethereum mainnet, and ETH as the output token on Unichain. Check it out on the Uniswap Interface. Bridge with a Partner Interface Many ecosystem partners offer bridges to Unichain. Check out the bridging page for details.
